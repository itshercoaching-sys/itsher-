The 8-week journey

What happens on The Narrow Path

Each week is a step. Each step requires truth. There is no skipping ahead.

Week
01
The Wound — Face What You Have Been Running From
We start where it actually started — not where you think it did. We name the wound, look at it directly, and stop pretending it is not there. No one heals from something they will not acknowledge. This week will be uncomfortable. That is the point.
"The thing you keep circling is the thing we are walking straight toward."
Week
02
The Old Identity — Who You Agreed to Be
Every pattern in your life was built by an identity you agreed to — consciously or not. We excavate it completely. The labels, the lies, the version of you that was shaped by what happened to you instead of who you actually are.
"You cannot build a new life on an identity that was never truly yours."
Week
03
The Chains — What Is Actually Holding You
Soul ties. Strongholds. Cycles that repeat no matter how many times you try to break them. We identify what has ground in your life and exactly how it got there. This is the week people understand why nothing has worked before.
"You cannot break free from something you have not yet named."
Week
04
First 1:1 Session — Your Private Breakthrough
Your first private session with her. Just you. No group, no audience. Everything you have uncovered in the first three weeks brought into one conversation. She will see what the group sessions cannot reach.
"Some things only break in private."
Week
05
The Wilderness — Why the Gap Exists
The gap between where you are and where you know you should be has a reason. We map it — not to mourn it, but to extract what it is trying to teach you before it releases you. The wilderness is not punishment. It is preparation.
"The wilderness ends when the lesson finally lands."
Week
06
The True Identity — Who You Actually Are
This is where we build — not rebuild. Who you actually are was never the damaged version. We go back to the original design: who you were created to be before the world got to work on you. This is the foundation everything from here will stand on.
"You are not your story. You are what existed before the story began."
Week
07
The Purpose — Why You Are Still Here
Not a general answer. A specific one. We look at your gifts, your scars, your whole story — and find the thread that connects everything you have been through to everything you are supposed to build. Your survival was not an accident. It was an assignment.
"Your survival was not luck. It was a mandate."
Week
08
Second 1:1 + The Path Forward
Your final private session. We close the 8 weeks, mark what has changed, and map exactly what you are walking into. You leave with one clear, specific next step — not a feeling, not a vague direction. A concrete move you can make this week.
"This is not the end. This is the first step on the right road."
